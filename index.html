            05 Safety

            If something goes wrong

            • 911Emergency: police, fire, ambulance. Text-to-911 works in many Georgia counties; call if you can.
            • 988Suicide and crisis lifeline, call or text, any hour.
            • 211United Way: rides, food, cooling and warming centers, senior services.
            • 311City of Atlanta: broken sidewalks, curb ramps, signals.
            • MARTA MobilityDoor-to-door rides if you cannot use buses or trains. Get the current phone numbers and how to apply on MARTA's official page.
            • MARTA Police404-848-4911 emergency line, as listed in MARTA's ride guide. On a train or at a station you can also use the white call box.

            Heat and cold

            In hot weather: choose morning, drink water before you are thirsty, rest in a library or a store. In freezing weather: watch for ice, wear layers, and ask 211 about warming centers. Call 911 for confusion, fainting, or a very high body temperature.

            About

            What this is

            MARTA Max is a student project for the TSA AI Hackathon. It helps older adults and people with disabilities find places they can actually use, at a time of day that is safe and comfortable, and get there more easily.

            Data

            Open sources, shown honestly

            MARTA stop accessibility (MARTA schedule data), places and wheelchair tags (OpenStreetMap volunteers), weather (Open-Meteo, 2021–2025), county lines (US Census). "Not reported" means nobody has recorded it, not that it is inaccessible. Always call first.

            Limits

            What we do not claim

            This is a prototype, not medical or legal advice. Hours and access can change, and elevators break. Heat figures are historical averages, not a forecast. The helper is an AI and can be wrong. AI helped write this site's code; people check the sources.
